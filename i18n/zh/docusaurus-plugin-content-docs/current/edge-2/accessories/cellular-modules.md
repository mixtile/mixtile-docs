## \[QUECTEL EC25 4G module kit\]

### Product parameter

- **model**
  - EC25-V Mini PCIe
- **supply voltage**
  - 3.0V~ 3.6V, typical value: 3.3V
- **operating frequency**
  - GSM: 850 MHz
  - EGSM: 900 MHz
  - DCS: 1800 MHz
  - PCS: 1900 MHz
  - WCDMA: B1/B2/B4/B5/B6/B8/B19 MHz
  - LTE-FDD: B1/B2/B3/B4/B5/B7/B8/B12/B13/B14/B18/B19/B20/B26/B28/B66/B71 MHz
  - LTE-TDD: B38/B40/B41 MHz
- **data transmission**
  - TDD-LTE： Max 150Mbps (DL) Max 50Mbps (UL)
  - FDD-LTE： Max 130Mbps (DL) Max 30Mbps (UL)
  - UMTS： Max 42Mbps (DL) Max 5.76Mbps (UL)
  - WCDMA： Max 384Kbps (DL) Max 384Kbps (UL)
  - EDGE： Max 296Kbps (DL) Max 236.8Kbps (UL)
  - GPRS： Max 107Kbps (DL) Max 85.6Kbps (UL)
- **interface connector**
  - USB：USB 2.0 high speed interface, 480Mbps
  - Digital voice：1 digital voice interface
  - USIM/SIM：1.8V/3V
  - UART：×1 UART
  - PCM：x1
  - Antenna：×3 (main atenna, diversity antenna and GNSS antenna interface)
- **structure size**
  - 51.0mm × 30.0mm × 4.9mm
- **weight**
  - about 9.8g
- **certificate**
  - RoHS

## \[QUECTEL RM500Q 5G module kit\]

### Product parameter

- **model**
  - RM500Q-AE
- **supply voltage**
  - 3.135V~ 4.4V, typical value: 3.7V
- **operating frequency**
  - 5G NR: 3GPP Release 15 NSA/SA operation, Sub-6 GHz
  - 5G NR NSA: n1/n2/n3/n5/n7/n8/n12/n20/n25/n28/n38/n40/n41/n48\*/n66/n71/n77/n78/n79
  - 5G NR SA: n1/n2/n3/n5/n7/n8/n12/n20/n25/n28/n38/n40/n41/n48\*/n66/n71/n77/n78/n79
  - WCDMA: B1/B2/B3/B4/B5/B6/B8/B19
  - LTE-FDD: B1/B2/B3/B4/B5/B7/B8/B12(B17)/B13/B14/B18/B19/B20/B25/B26/B28/B29/B30/B32/B66/B71
  - LTE-TDD: B34/B38/B39/B40/B41/B42/B43/B48
- **data transmission**
  - 5G SA Sub-6： Max 2.1Gbps (DL) Max 450Mbps (UL)
  - 5G NSA Sub-6： Max 2.5Gbps (DL) Max 650Mbps (UL)
  - LTE： Max 1.0Gbps (DL) Max 200Mbps (UL)
  - WCDMA： Max 42Mbps (DL) Max 5.76Mbps (UL)
- **interface connector**
  - (U)SIM：×1

  - PCIe 3.0: x1
  - PCM: x1
  - Antenna interface: ×4
- **structure size**

- **weight**
