# U.2

The U.2 connector is a 68-pin SFF-8639 connector on the short edge of the board, opposite the DC jack. It carries interfaces for expansion boards:

- Two SATA 3.0 channels, SATA0 and SATA2. The SATA2 lanes can carry PCIe 2.0 instead.
- An RGMII interface to the second Ethernet controller, GMAC0, for an external Ethernet PHY
- A USB 2.0 port
- I²C3
- 5 V, up to 3 A

![Top view of the Edge 2 board with the U.2 connector marked on the short edge](/img/docs/edge-2-board/edge-2-u2-connector.webp)

_Figure 1. The U.2 connector_

The U.2 connector is intended for custom expansion boards. Using its interfaces needs support in the operating system. For the status in Mixtile OS, see [Hardware Interfaces in Mixtile OS](/edge-2/system-usage#hardware-interfaces-in-mixtile-os).

The SATA power connector, on the opposite short edge next to the DC jack, supplies a SATA drive attached through the U.2 connector, and can also power the board, see [Power Supply](/edge-2/getting-started/power-supply).

### SATA power connector

On the SATA power connector, pins 13 to 15 carry 12 V and pins 10 to 12 are ground.
